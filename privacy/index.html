Privacy Policy

Imposter Game Night · Last updated October 5, 2026

Imposter Game Night is a party game played on one device. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not send your rounds anywhere. This page describes the little data the app handles and why. If the app changes, this page changes with it.

What stays on your device

The player names you type, the decks you write yourself and the history of rounds — how many you played, who was caught — are written to the app's own storage on your phone. They are never uploaded, never shared and never seen by us. Deleting the app removes all of it.

What the app sends

Nothing about your game. The app makes no network request while you deal, reveal, discuss or vote. It works in full with the phone offline.

One exception, and it stands between you and a company rather than between you and us:

  • Purchases. If you open the subscription screen or buy, Apple and RevenueCat handle the payment. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier for your device and the state of your subscription. It never receives your names, your words or anything else you typed into the app.

Analytics

There are none. The app contains no analytics service and reports no events anywhere. If that ever changes, this page will name the service that receives them before the version that sends them ships.

Children

The app collects nothing that could identify anyone, of any age. The words in every deck are everyday nouns.

Your rights

Because nothing leaves the device, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. Deleting the app deletes everything it ever stored.
