Imposter Game Night

A party word game for one phone and a table of friends.

One phone goes round the table. Everyone sees the same secret word — except one of you. Describe it in a sentence without saying it, argue, and vote: who is the imposter?

How it plays

Each player holds a finger on the screen to see their word, and the moment they lift it the word is gone. No card is left face-up for the next person to read.

Three modes: the imposter knows only the category, knows nothing at all, or gets a word close to the real one and does not know they are the imposter. Three to twenty players, one to three imposters, with or without a discussion timer.

Decks

Six decks — party, food, places, football, films and jobs — and you can write your own: type the words one per line and play with them the same evening.

No internet, no account

The game runs entirely on the device. There is no online mode, no sign-up and nothing to create. It works with the phone in airplane mode.
