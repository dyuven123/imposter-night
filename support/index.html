The word disappeared before I read it

Keep your finger on the panel. The word shows only while held, so that nobody can pick the phone up afterwards and read what you had.

How many players do I need?

Three at the least. A second imposter needs five at the table and a third needs seven — otherwise the imposters are not outnumbered and the vote cannot resolve.

We ran out of words

Open Decks and write your own: one word per line, ten words minimum. It stays on your phone and plays like any other deck.

I paid and the decks are still locked

Open the subscription screen and tap Restore. If they are still locked, write to us with the email address of the Apple ID you paid with.

What does the subscription unlock?

Three of the six decks. The other three are free with no time limit, and one paid deck is open to everyone each day — the game is fully playable without paying.

Is there an online mode?

No, and it is not planned. The game is built for people in one room sharing one phone.
